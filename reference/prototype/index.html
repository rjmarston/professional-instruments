Precision Engineering and Manufacturing Services

For work outside the standard line, or parts made to the same tolerances as our spindles.

Precision engineering

When a specification falls outside our standard lines, our engineers design and build a spindle for the application. Custom spindle making at PI goes back almost to the beginning in 1946.

  • Custom air bearing spindles, designed to the application
  • Hemispherical, dual diameter, air pad and vacuum-rated designs
  • Aluminum, Invar, graphite and ceramic structures
  • Cost-effective from 25 units
