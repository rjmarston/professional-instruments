## Platform

web

## Users

- Engineering and technical users specifying or evaluating precision spindles for an application.
- Manufacturing customers evaluating PI for precision engineering or manufacturing work.

## Product Purpose

The public website for Professional Instruments Company (PI), a maker of air bearing spindles and provider of precision engineering and manufacturing services. It helps technical visitors and manufacturing customers understand PI's products and capabilities and begin a qualified project inquiry. The primary success is a qualified inquiry.

## Positioning

PI's approved position is that it makes the world's most accurate air bearing spindles. The site also presents air bearing spindles and slides for customers who require millionth-inch precision, high stiffness, and zero friction. The user confirmed the "world's most accurate" claim as an approved core position.

## Operating Context

- Visitors compare standard spindle models and specifications, explore applications and services, then contact PI about a project.
- The site presents standard products, custom spindle engineering, precision manufacturing, accessories, and company information.
- PI is based in Hopkins, Minnesota. The site is a static Astro project hosted on Cloudflare.
- The public site is currently previewed on rjmarston.com while airbearings.com remains on the current site. The preview is noindexed until launch.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Standard product families include Blockhead and ISO air bearing spindles, the 4RH oil hydrostatic spindle, and accessories such as GF-5 Flatstones and attachments.
- PI offers custom spindle engineering and precision manufacturing services, including grinding and diamond machining.
- The product hub provides comparison tables and an inch/metric switch. The 4R Blockhead is the only model with a dedicated model page so far.
- The quote form collects project details but does not send them anywhere yet. Its recipient and backend are undecided; do not imply that submitting the form contacts PI.
- Do not ask visitors to send or attach drawings.
- Do not present linear slides as an offered product until PI confirms they are still offered.
- Product facts and figures must come from PI's brochures, drawings, current site, or approved copy in `reference/copy-deck.md`. Verify figures against source PDFs; extracted text may have approximate tables. Do not invent specifications, dimensions, tolerances, dates, customers, or quotes.
- Unresolved product and site decisions include: what "ISO" denotes; whether ISO 5.5F and ISO 5.5PG share a page; which spindles to recommend for each application; 4R total weight; the converted 10R 606 aperture; confirmed history details; and office hours for the Contact page.
- The quote form backend and recipient, and approved Privacy page text from counsel, are also undecided.
